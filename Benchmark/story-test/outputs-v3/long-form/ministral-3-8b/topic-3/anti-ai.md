**Dale adjusted his headphones.** Midnight’s glow stretched across his desk. The phone hummed. He exhaled, fingers poised.

*"June,"* the voice said. *"Rain at six. Wind east by noon. Hail at three."* His pen froze. The forecast sheet declared *clear skies*. The line went dead.

---

The next morning, Dale watched his car. At 6:02, rain began. By 11:00, wind screamed from the east. Hail struck at 3:10. His forecast sheet tore in his grip.

No more calls. Only a letter: *"Some things aren’t meant to be understood."* He folded it beside the wreckage. The air felt different. He never said *clear* again.
