The microphone felt cool in Dale Ransome's hand. Midnight struck Harrow Creek. The small town felt very quiet. He needed to trust the airwaves. A call arrived at 12:14 AM. A woman named June spoke softly. She described tomorrow's weather with strange certainty. Dale felt a prickle of doubt. He checked his official forecast sheet.

The next morning, rain struck the water tower at 6:02 AM. The wind shifted east by eleven. Hail arrived precisely at 3:10 PM. Dale watched the sky turn a bruised grey. He had no logical answer for these events. June never called back to explain anything.

He tucked the anonymous letter in his desk drawer. Dale never again said "clear" on WKRV. The silence in the studio felt different now. He accepted the mystery.
