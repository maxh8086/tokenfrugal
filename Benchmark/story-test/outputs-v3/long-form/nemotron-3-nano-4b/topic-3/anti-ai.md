Dale sat at his desk in the quiet studio of WKRV at 12:14. The air smelled of old paper and static. He had run the midnight show for eleven years. He took every call seriously.  
June called. She said the rain would start at the water tower at six. The wind would turn east by noon. The hail would come after three.  
Dale checked the forecast sheet. It said clear skies.  
He thought about that for a while. The studio hummed.  
At the end of the day he decided to play the call the next morning.  
The next day the rain began at six:02 at the water tower. The wind turned east by eleven. The hail came at three:10.  
Dale sat in his car in the station lot and watched the sky turn grey.  
He had no explanation. He did not pretend to have one.  
June never called back. The station received a few emails. A flood of questions. One letter with no return address.  
Dale kept the letter in his drawer. Next to the old forecast sheet.  
He never again said the word clear on air.
