**Act 1: Setup**

Harrow Creek's WKRV midnight show is trusted after dark. Host Dale Ransome, with eleven years of experience, takes every call seriously. At 12:14, a call comes in from June. She claims to describe tomorrow's weather. Dale is skeptical but listens as she predicts specific events with calm precision.

**Act 2: Conflict**

The studio buzzes with anticipation. Dale checks the forecast sheet, which reads clear skies. He ponders this for a while. The next day, rain begins at 6:02 at the water tower, wind turns east by eleven, and hail comes at 3:10. Dale watches the sky turn grey in his car, having no explanation for June's uncanny prediction.

**Act 3: Resolution**

Dale decides not to say "clear" on air again. June never calls back, but the station receives emails, questions, and one letter with no return address. Dale keeps the letter next to the old forecast sheet, a testament to the inexplicable event that challenged his skepticism.
