What if your neighborhood's shared water tank became a mystery? In Port Marrow, Mara read a letter from Lindqvist Partners on Grey Stair. They wanted to manage the tank. Mara felt a knot in her stomach.

At the Thursday meeting, everyone gathered. Old Tobin said he saw this coming. Priya suggested a lawyer. Mara stayed quiet, then spoke. "We have the tank key, pump receipts, and nine years of meter readings. That counts for something."

They decided to share the truth. They photographed the meters, copied the receipts, and posted the timeline on the stairwell wall. By the weekend, the whole street knew.
