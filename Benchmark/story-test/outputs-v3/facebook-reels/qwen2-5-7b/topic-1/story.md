What if your neighborhood's shared water tank suddenly became a mystery? In Port Marrow, on Grey Stair, Mara read a letter from Lindqvist Partners. They wanted to manage the tank. Mara felt a knot in her stomach.

At the Thursday meeting, everyone gathered around the tea urn. Old Tobin said he saw this coming. Priya suggested a lawyer. Mara stayed quiet, then spoke. "We have the tank key, the pump receipts, and nine years of meter readings. That counts for something."

They decided to share the truth. They photographed the meters, copied the receipts, and posted the timeline on the stairwell wall. By the weekend, the whole street knew.

Nostalgia for the good old days, isn't it?
