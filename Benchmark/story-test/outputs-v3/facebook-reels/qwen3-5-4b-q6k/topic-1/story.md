Remember that dusty old water tank on the roof?
It held our water for three long, dry years.
We all shared that big concrete cistern above Grey Stair.
Then a letter arrived, changing everything for us all.
A developer bought the rights to our shared supply.
They said access would be reviewed in due course.
Mara felt a knot in her stomach that morning.
She knew this was a game-changer for our home.
At the Thursday meeting, tea steamed from the urn.
Old Tobin said he had seen this coming.
Priya suggested they call a lawyer for help.
Mara spoke up with nine years of meter readings.
She showed the pump receipts and the tank key.
The room went quiet as people listened to her.
They decided to shed light on the situation now.
They posted the timeline on the stairwell wall.
By the weekend, the whole street knew the truth.
We still share that feeling of standing together.
