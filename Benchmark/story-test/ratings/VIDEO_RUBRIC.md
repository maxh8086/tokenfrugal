# Story-film rubric: LinkedIn Professional Impact Framework (200 points)

* **Source:** the owner's framework, supplied in chat on 2026-11-04.
* **Use:** Part 1 scores the script (`story.md`). Part 2 scores the rendered film (`story-film` output). Part 2 is the video rubric named in `docs/designs/story-model-test-plan.md` section 4.
* **Scope:** the framework is written for LinkedIn. The other destinations (Shorts, Reels, long-form) use the video column of the platform table in `docs/designs/story-to-video-workflows.md` section 5.1. That mapping is not scored here yet.
* **Output per scored film:** a score per category, a total out of 200 (or out of 100 for Part 2 alone), and five actionable tweaks.

## Part 1: Script and professional framing (100 points)

| # | Category | Max | What is checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | B2B hook | 30 | The first 3 seconds (about 8 words) carry a bold professional statement, a contrarian business thought, or a high-stakes future-of-work simulation. |
| 1.2 | Insight and value | 25 | The script gives a clear business lesson, parable or thought-leadership insight, not purely fictional entertainment. It is worth saving or bookmarking. |
| 1.3 | Text post alignment | 25 | The script lends itself to a text post with a strong hook in the first 210 characters that expands on the video's lesson. |
| 1.4 | Call to action and comment engine | 20 | The ending invites professional debate or asks for the audience's specific view. |

## Part 2: Video and execution (100 points)

| # | Category | Max | What is checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Visual polish and professionalism | 30 | The first frame looks like high-end documentary or commercial work, or a sleek capability showcase, not a bizarre or unpolished generation. |
| 2.2 | Muted viewing accessibility | 25 | Bold text overlays in the first 3 seconds. Captions are central, clean and professionally styled for the 85% who watch without sound. |
| 2.3 | Pacing and dwell time | 25 | Cuts are steady and deliberate, not hyper-stimulating. The video is strictly under 90 seconds. |
| 2.4 | Audio and tone | 20 | The voiceover is authoritative, clear and documentary-style, not panicked or extreme. |

## Scoring notes

* **Hard gate:** a film of 90 seconds or more fails 2.3 outright. The duration check in the pipeline enforces this.
* **Frame review:** categories 2.1 and 2.2 are scored from frame sheets (see section 5 of the design doc). Use the same reviewer for every film in a run.
* **Not scored offline:** trending platform audio (Reels, from the platform table) is excluded, because no licensed audio runs offline. Do not penalise a film for not having it.
* **Ties:** rank by the Part 2 total, then by Part 1 total.
