Remember those long summer afternoons? The heat in Port Marrow felt endless. Our rooftop garden co-op relied on one big tank. It fed all twelve flats. Then, a notice arrived. Lindqvist Partners bought the tank rights. Access would be "reviewed." Mara felt a sudden chill. At the Thursday meeting, tea warmed our hands. Tobin sighed. Priya suggested a lawyer. Mara spoke up then. "We have the keys. We have the records." The room went quiet. We shared our proof. We posted the timeline on the stairwell. The whole street knew by the weekend. Feeling that shared resilience brings such warmth.
