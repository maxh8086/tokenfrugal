Late at night, Dale Ransome sat in his dim studio, headphones on, listening to WKRV's midnight show. Harrow Creek relied on his voice for comfort. At 12:14, June predicted the weather. Dale scribbled notes, skepticism fading.

Rain began at 6:02, exactly as June said. By eleven, the wind shifted east. At 3:10, hail pounded the town. Dale watched, stunned, from his car. The forecast sheet, marked clear, lay crumpled.

Days passed. Emails flooded the station, questions about June’s accuracy. One anonymous letter arrived, cryptic. Dale kept it, alongside the old forecast sheet. He never again used the word "clear" on air.
