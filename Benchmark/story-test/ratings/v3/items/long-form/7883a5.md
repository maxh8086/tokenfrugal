Late in the night, Dale Ransome sat in his dimly lit studio, headphones on, listening to the static of WKRV's midnight show. The small inland town of Harrow Creek relied on his voice for comfort after dark. At 12:14, a call came in. June, her voice calm, predicted the weather for the next day. Dale scribbled notes, his skepticism waning.

The next morning, rain began at 6:02, exactly as June said. By eleven, the wind shifted east. At 3:10, hail pounded the town. Dale watched, stunned, from his car in the station lot. The forecast sheet on his desk, marked clear, lay crumpled.

Days passed. Emails flooded the station, questions about June’s accuracy. One anonymous letter arrived, its contents cryptic. Dale kept it, alongside the old forecast sheet. He never again used the word "clear" on air.
