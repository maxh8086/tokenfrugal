The microphone felt cool in Dale Ransome's hand. Midnight in Harrow Creek, the small town felt quiet. He needed to trust the airwaves. A call came in at 12:14 AM. A woman named June spoke softly. She described tomorrow's weather with strange certainty. Dale felt a prickle of doubt. He checked his official forecast sheet.

The next morning, the rain hit the water tower at 6:02 AM. The wind shifted east by eleven. Hail arrived precisely at 3:10 PM. Dale watched the sky turn a bruised grey. He had no logical answer for the events. June never called back to explain.

He tucked the anonymous letter into his desk drawer. Dale never again uttered the word "clear" on WKRV. The silence in the studio felt different now.
