**Act 1: Setup**

In Harrow Creek, WKRV's midnight show is the trusted voice after dark. Host Dale Ransome, with eleven years of experience, takes every call seriously, even the strange ones. At 12:14, a call comes in from June, who claims to describe tomorrow's weather. Dale is skeptical but listens as she predicts specific weather events with calm precision.

**Act 2: Conflict**

The studio buzzes with anticipation. Dale checks the forecast sheet, which reads clear skies. He ponders this for a while. The next day, the rain begins at 6:02 at the water tower, the wind turns east by eleven, and the hail comes at 3:10. Dale watches the sky turn grey in his car, having no explanation for June's uncanny prediction.

**Act 3: Resolution**

Dale decides not to say "clear" on air again. June never calls back, but the station receives emails, questions, and one letter with no return address. Dale keeps the letter next to the old forecast sheet, a testament to the inexplicable event that challenged his skepticism.
