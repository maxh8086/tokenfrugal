Dale Ransome sat alone in the dark studio of WKRV, Harrow Creek, at midnight. He had hosted this show for eleven years, taking every call seriously, even the strange ones. His motive was simple: to be the trusted voice for his small town after dark.

A woman named June called at 12:14, claiming she knew tomorrow's weather. She spoke calmly, listing rain at the water tower, east winds, and hail. Dale felt a chill run down his spine as he checked the clear skies on his desk.

The next morning, the rain began exactly at six. The wind turned east by eleven, and hail struck at three. Dale watched the grey sky from his car, knowing no explanation existed for the perfect prediction.

June never called back, leaving only a mysterious letter with no return address. Dale kept it in his drawer next to the old forecast sheet. He realized the town needed protection from forces beyond his control. Dale stopped predicting weather, choosing silence instead of risking another disaster. The mystery of June's power remained, but the town felt safer without his words.
