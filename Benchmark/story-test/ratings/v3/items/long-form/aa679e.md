Dale Ransome sat alone in the WKRV studio at midnight. The clock read twelve-fifteen. He wanted to hear one more voice before sleep took him. The phone rang at twelve-fifteen-four. A woman named June spoke softly. She said rain would start at the water tower at six. Dale felt a chill run down his spine. He asked her how she knew. June said the wind would turn east by noon. She said hail would come just after three. Dale checked his weather sheet. It showed clear skies. He felt a strange doubt in his chest. He decided to play the call tomorrow morning. The next day, rain fell at six-fifty-two. The wind shifted east by eleven. Hail struck the ground at three-ten. Dale sat in his car watching the grey sky. He had no explanation for the storm. June never called back again. He kept her letter in his drawer. He never said the word clear on air. The silence filled the room. He listened to the rain outside. The storm had changed everything. He finally understood the truth.
