**Dale adjusted his headphones.** Midnight’s desk bathed in eerie blue light. The phone buzzed. He paused—breath held.

*"June,"* the voice whispered. *"Rain at six. Wind shifts east. Hail by three."* His pen hovered. The printed forecast mocked him—*sunny*. The line clicked dead.

---

Morning came. His car stood motionless. At 6:02, rain lashed windows. By noon, wind howled from the east. Hail pelted at 3:10. The forecast sheet shredded in his fist.

No more calls. Just a letter: *"Some truths resist explanation."* He tucked it beside the wreckage. The air hummed with warning. Silence replaced his old certainty.
