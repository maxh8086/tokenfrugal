**Act 1: Setup**

In Harrow Creek, WKRV's midnight show is a trusted beacon. Dale Ransome, the seasoned host, has been leading for eleven years, taking every call seriously. One cold night at 12:14, a call comes in from June, who claims to predict tomorrow's weather with uncanny accuracy. She foretells rain at the water tower at six, a shift in wind to the east by noon, and hail at three. Her calm demeanor adds an eerie weight to her words.

**Act 2: Conflict**

Dale, skeptical yet intrigued, notes a shift in the room during her call. He checks his forecast sheet, which reads clear skies. A moment of contemplation follows, and he decides to air her call the next morning. The following day, the town's weather aligns perfectly with June's predictions. The rain starts at six, the wind shifts east by eleven, and hail hits at three. Dale watches the sky turn grey, his earlier doubts now unshakable.

**Act 3: Resolution**

June never returns, leaving behind a few emails and a letter with no return address. Dale keeps the letter beside his old forecast sheet. From then on, he never again utters the word "clear" on air, marking a new era of cautious optimism in Harrow Creek's late-night radio.
