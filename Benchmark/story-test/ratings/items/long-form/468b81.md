**FORECAST CALLER**
*Three-Act Script*

---

### **ACT I: SETUP**
*(The air in Harrow Creek smells of damp pine. WKRV’s studio flickers—neon "LIVE" cracked. Dale Ransome adjusts his headphones. The clock ticks toward midnight.)*

**DALE** *(into mic, tired)*
"Harrow Creek, this is Dale. If the sky’s wrong tonight, you’re not alone."

*(Static. Then—)*

**JUNE** *(voice sharp)*
"Six AM. Water tower. Rain starts there."

*(Dale blinks. His sheet says "Clear. 72°." He exhales.)*

**DALE** *(doubtful)*
"Ma’am, the weatherman says—"

**JUNE**
"Wind shifts east by noon. Hail at three."

*(Dead line. Dale scribbles notes. Outside, a coyote yips. The fan wheezes.)*

---

### **ACT II: CONFLICT**
*(Dawn breaks. Dale’s car sits untouched. Sky stays blue—until 6:02 AM. First drop hits glass. By noon, wind howls. At 3:10 PM, hail smashes his roof. He doesn’t move. The radio screams—voices panic—but he ignores it. The storm ignores him.)*

*(Later, a letter arrives. No stamp. No name. Ink smells like wet earth. One line: "You’re not the first to listen." Dale’s hands shake. The town’s silence feels heavier.)*

---
### **ACT III: RESOLUTION**
*(Dale stands in the empty studio. The forecast sheet lies beside the letter. He picks up the phone. Dead line. He hangs up. Then—)*

**DALE** *(quiet)*
"Tomorrow? Tomorrow, we’ll see."

*(He doesn’t say "clear" again. The radio turns on. The storm’s last echoes fade. The air holds its breath.)*
