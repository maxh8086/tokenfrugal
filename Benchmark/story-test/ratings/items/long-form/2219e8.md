**Act 1: Setup**

The small town of Harrow Creek was bathed in darkness, the only sound the hum of WKRV's midnight show. Host Dale Ransome had run the show for eleven years. At 12:14, a call came in from a woman named June. Dale's curiosity was piqued as June described tomorrow's weather with eerie accuracy.

**Act 2: Conflict**

Dale checked the forecast sheet on his desk, but it said clear skies. He felt a shiver run down his spine as he thought about the discrepancy. The studio hummed, and Dale's mind raced with questions. Why was June so confident? Was she a prankster or something more? He decided to play the call the next morning.

**Act 3: Resolution**

The next day, the rain began at 6:02 at the water tower, the wind turned east by eleven, and the hail came at 3:10. Dale sat in his car, watching the sky turn grey. He had no explanation. June never called back, but the station received a flood of questions. Dale kept the letter in his drawer, next to the old forecast sheet, and never again said the word "clear" on air.
