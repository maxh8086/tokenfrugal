**ACT ONE: SETUP**
The studio hummed with static. Dale Ransome stared at the clear skies forecast. He had hosted WKRV for eleven years. Every call mattered to him. A woman named June spoke calmly. She listed rain at the water tower. Wind would shift east by noon. Hail would strike just after three. Dale felt a strange chill in the air. He checked his paper again. It still said sunny.

**ACT TWO: CONFLICT**
Dale hesitated. He considered ignoring the bizarre prediction. But curiosity pulled him forward. He decided to play the tape. The next morning, dawn broke grey. At six minutes past six, the water tower rained. Dale watched the sky turn dark. By eleven, the wind howled from the east. At three ten, hail shattered the windows. Dale sat in his car. He had no explanation for the storm. He did not pretend to know why.

**ACT THREE: RESOLUTION**
June never called back again. The station flooded with emails and questions. One letter arrived with no return address. Dale kept it in his drawer. It sat next to the old sheet. He never said "clear" on air. The town trusted him again. The mystery remained unsolved. Dale drove home in silence. The storm had changed everything.
