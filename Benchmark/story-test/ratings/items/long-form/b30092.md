**Act 1: Setup**

*INT. WKRV STUDIO - NIGHT*

*The clock reads 12:14. The radio hums softly. DALE RANSOME, mid-40s, sits at his desk, surrounded by weather maps and forecast sheets.*

DALE
(whispering to himself)
Another night, another call.

*The phone rings. DALE answers.*

DALE
(voice calm)
WKRV, Dale Ransome speaking.

*June's voice, steady and detached, comes through the line.*

JUNE
I can describe tomorrow's weather.

DALE
(skeptical)
Really? Let's hear it.

*June describes the weather with eerie accuracy. DALE checks his forecast sheet, which says clear skies.*

DALE
(still skeptical)
You're pretty confident.

JUNE
(calm)
Just stating facts.

*DALE feels a chill. He nods to himself, deciding to play the call the next morning.*

**Act 2: Conflict**

*INT. WKRV STUDIO - NEXT MORNING*

*The studio is bustling. DALE sits in his car in the station lot, watching the sky turn grey.*

DALE
(to himself)
How did she know?

*The radio plays the call. The studio audience listens in silence.*

ANNOUNCER
And now, a call from June, who claims to know tomorrow's weather.

*The studio hums. DALE watches the sky, his mind racing.*

*The call plays. The weather happens exactly as June predicted. DALE sits in his car, stunned.*

DALE
(whispering)
No explanation.

*The station receives a flood of emails and one anonymous letter. DALE keeps the letter, next to the old forecast sheet.*

**Act 3: Resolution**

*INT. WKRV STUDIO - NIGHT*

*DALE sits alone, holding the letter. He looks at the old forecast sheet.*

DALE
(to himself)
Clear skies.

*He shakes his head, a mix of disbelief and respect.*

DALE
(whispering)
June never called back.

*DALE keeps the letter, a reminder of the night the weather changed. He never again says "clear" on air.*

*FADE OUT.*
