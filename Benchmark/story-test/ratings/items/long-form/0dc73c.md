**FORECAST CALLER**
*(Three-Act Script – Final Voice Pass)*

---

### **ACT I: SETUP**
*(Neon "LIVE" flickers. Dale adjusts headphones. Harrow Creek’s damp pine scent lingers.)*

**DALE** *(into mic, weary)*
"Harrow Creek. Dale here. If the sky’s wrong tonight..."

*(Static hums. Then—)*

**JUNE** *(sharp, urgent)*
"Six AM. Water tower. Rain starts there."

*(Dale hesitates. His notes say "Clear. 72°." He exhales.)*

**DALE** *(skeptical)*
"Ma’am, the weatherman says—"

**JUNE**
"Wind shifts east by noon. Hail at three."

*(Dead line. Outside, a coyote howls. The fan groans.)*

---

### **ACT II: CONFLICT**
*(Dawn breaks. Dale’s car remains untouched. Sky stays blue—until 6:02 AM. First raindrop hits glass. Wind screams by noon. At 3:10 PM, hail pounds his roof. He stays frozen. Radio chaos fades—he doesn’t listen. The storm doesn’t care.)*

*(Later—a letter arrives. No stamp. No name. Ink smells like wet earth. One line: "You’re not the first to listen." Dale’s hands tremble. The town’s silence presses down.)*

---
### **ACT III: RESOLUTION**
*(Dale stands in the empty studio. Forecast sheet lies beside the letter. He picks up the phone. Dead line. He hangs up. Then—)*

**DALE** *(soft, resolved)*
"Tomorrow? Tomorrow, we’ll see."

*(No "clear" this time. Radio hums. Storm’s echoes fade. The air waits.)*
