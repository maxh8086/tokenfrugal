Act 1: Setup

WKRV's midnight show is the only voice many trust after dark in Harrow Creek. Host Dale Ransome, who has run the show for eleven years, takes every call seriously. The studio hums with anticipation as Dale prepares for the next call.

Act 2: Conflict

At 12:14, a call comes in from a woman who identifies herself only as June. She describes tomorrow's weather: rain starting at the water tower at six in the morning, wind turning east by noon, and hail coming just after three. Dale's eyes widen as he checks the forecast sheet on his desk, which says clear skies. He debates whether to play the call the next morning.

Act 3: Resolution

The next day, the rain begins at 6:02 at the water tower, the wind turns east by eleven, and the hail comes at 3:10. Dale sits in his car in the station lot, watching the sky turn grey. He has no explanation for June's accurate predictions and does not pretend to have one. June never calls back. The station receives a few emails, a flood of questions, and one letter with no return address. Dale keeps the letter in his drawer, next to the old forecast sheet, and he never again says the word "clear" on air.
