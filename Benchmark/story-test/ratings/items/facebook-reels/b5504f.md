It happened in Port Marrow, where drought lasted three years.

Our rooftop garden co-op on Grey Stair felt every drop.

We all shared one big concrete tank on the roof.

That tank fed all twelve flats in our building.

Then a letter arrived, and Mara read it twice.

A developer named Lindqvist Partners bought the tank rights.

The letter was polite, but it felt like a shift.

At the Thursday meeting, everyone gathered around the tea urn.

Old Tobin said he saw this coming long ago.

Priya suggested they call a lawyer immediately.

Mara stayed quiet for a while, then spoke up.

She showed them the tank key and nine years of receipts.

The room went silent, listening to her steady voice.

They decided to fight back with proof and photos.

They posted the timeline on the stairwell wall for all to see.

By the weekend, the whole street knew what was happening.

We still have our water, and our community feels safe.
