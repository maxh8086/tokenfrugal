Remember when the water tank was our shared lifeline?

Three years of drought hit Port Marrow hard.

The rooftop garden on Grey Stair felt every drop.

Twelve families drank from one big concrete cistern.

Then a letter arrived from Lindqvist Partners.

They wanted to "manage" the tank going forward.

Mara Ellis read it twice before speaking up.

She felt a tight knot in her stomach.

The Thursday meeting was full of worried voices.

Old Tobin said he saw this coming long ago.

Priya wanted to call a lawyer immediately.

Mara stayed quiet until she found her words.

"We have the key, the receipts, and nine years of readings."

The room went silent and listened closely.

They decided to show the truth to everyone.

Photos of the meters went on the stairwell wall.

Receipts were copied and pinned to the boards.

By the weekend, the whole street knew the story.

The neighbors shared a glass of water together.

Sometimes the simplest things hold us all together.
