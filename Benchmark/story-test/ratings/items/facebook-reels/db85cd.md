What if your shared water tank suddenly wasn't shared anymore? 🤔 In Port Marrow, a coastal city, drought hit for three years. 🌧️ The rooftop garden co-op on Grey Stair relied on the big concrete cistern. 💧

Mara read the notice twice. Lindqvist Partners bought the tank rights. "Managed going forward," it said. Mara's stomach tightened. 🤓

Thursday meeting. Tea urns clinked. Old Tobin said he saw this coming. Priya suggested a lawyer. Mara stayed quiet. Then, "We have the tank key, pump receipts, nine years of meter readings." 📊

The room went quiet. People listened. They decided to act. Photos of meters, copies of receipts, timeline on the stairwell. By weekend, the whole street knew. 📷
