Did you know? A retired topologist, Helena Voss, discovered an unproven lemma that could revolutionize knot theory. She called it the invariant. Her former student, Tomas Reyes, found it beautiful. The lemma, about boundary surfaces, was marked "clearly" and left unproven. Tomas tested it on a train, finding it held for every example. They raced to prove it, exchanging messages and sketches. The proof was a step, not what was imagined. The lemma is no longer a gap. Share this with the friend who loves math!
