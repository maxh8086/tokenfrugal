You sign off on a manifest. You trust the system. You fly the route you know.

Then, four sealed boxes appear. They are not on your list. The screen calls them "reserved cargo."

Captain Ines Varga felt a shift. She checked the seals. She checked the logs. She wrote down every timestamp. The boxes hummed faintly inside the cabin.

She asked the loader. He shrugged. He said it was not his place to ask.

She asked the dispatcher. He said the route was approved at a higher level.

Ines sat in the cockpit. She thought about that for a long time. At the end of the day, she was the one flying the aircraft. She was the one who would answer if something went wrong.

She filed a report. It was short. It was honest. Then she waited for a reply that did not come.

The lesson is clear: when your name is on the manifest, you own the risk.

What happens when your signature is required, but your authority is removed?
