1. **Vague Detail on the Developer's Identity**: The developer is referred to only as "Lindqvist Partners" without any explanation of who they are, their connection to the city, or why they specifically targeted this building, leaving the threat feeling ungrounded.
2. **Unexplained Legal Leverage**: Mara claims to have "nine years of meter readings" and receipts that prove the co-op's usage, yet the story never establishes why these documents would legally prevent a developer from purchasing the rights to the tank or why the developer ignored them initially.
3. **Absurdity of the "Public Matter" Strategy**: The co-op decides to turn a private property dispute into a "public matter" by posting a timeline on a stairwell wall; this is presented as a strategic move to gain leverage, but the story offers no reason why posting a paper on a wall would actually stop a corporation from evicting them or seizing the asset.
4. **Lack of Consequence for the Meeting**: The meeting concludes with a decision to post flyers, but the story provides no outcome or reaction from the developer, leaving the central conflict unresolved and the "strategy" feeling like a non-sequitur.
5. **Inconsistent Tone of the Ending**: The final paragraph shifts from a tense legal/social struggle to a poetic, almost inspirational declaration about the "spirit of the co-op" being "no longer dry," which feels disconnected from the gritty, realistic tone of the drought and eviction threat.
6. **No Reason for the Developer's Inaction**: After Mara presents evidence and the community posts a notice, the story implies the developer is powerless without showing any specific reason why the corporation would suddenly stop or be deterred by a wall poster.
7. **Vague Detail on the Tank's Mechanics**: The story mentions the tank is "perched precariously" and has "pipes feeding every flat," but never explains how the water actually gets there, how it is stored, or what happens if the pipes are cut, making the physical stakes unclear.
8. **Choice with No Reason for the Camera**: Mara takes a camera to photograph meters and receipts in a building where the primary issue is water scarcity; the story does not explain why photography is the chosen method of evidence gathering when the issue is a physical resource, not a visual one.
9. **Unmotivated Shift in Group Dynamics**: The room instantly shifts from "panic" to "strategy" solely because Mara spoke; the story does not show how the other twelve families, who were previously described as arguing and fearful, suddenly became cohesive strategists without further development of their personalities or motivations.
10. **Missing Stakes for the "Eviction"**: The letter says access will be "reviewed," but the story treats this as an immediate eviction of their lifeline without explaining what happens if the review is denied or if the tank is drained, leaving the reader unsure of the actual danger.
