In Port Marrow, a coastal city, water was a precious resource. The rooftop-garden co-op on Grey Stair had felt the drought for three years. A notice arrived, Mara read it twice. Lindqvist Partners had purchased the rights to the tank. Mara felt a knot in her stomach. This felt like a game-changer.

At the Thursday meeting, everyone had something to say. Old Tobin said he had seen this coming. Priya suggested calling a lawyer. Mara remained silent, then spoke. "We have the tank key, pump receipts, and nine years of meter readings. That counts for something." The room went quiet. People listened.

They decided to shed light on the situation. They photographed the meters, copied the receipts, and posted the timeline on the stairwell wall. By the weekend, the whole street knew.
