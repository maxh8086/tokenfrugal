[Opening shot: Dale Ransome, host of WKRV's midnight show, sits in his studio.]

Dale Ransome: Midnight radio, Harrow Creek. Trust in the voice of the night.

[Cut to: A caller, June, on the phone.]

June: Tomorrow's weather, rain starts at the water tower.

[Cut to: Dale looking skeptical, checking the forecast sheet.]

Dale: Rain at six, wind east by noon, hail after three.

[Cut to: Dale's face shifting from skepticism to contemplation.]

Dale: Clear skies on the forecast sheet.

[Cut to: The studio humming, Dale's thoughts visible.]

Dale: Play the call tomorrow.

[Cut to: The next morning, Dale playing the call.]

June: Rain at 6:02, wind east by eleven, hail at 3:10.

[Cut to: Dale watching the sky turn grey in his car.]

Dale: No explanation, no pretense.

[Cut to: Dale looking at the old forecast sheet and the letter.]

Dale: June never called back.

[Cut to: Dale's final line echoing the opening line.]

Dale: Clear skies, or not.
