Four sealed boxes appeared on a medical drone.
They hummed with a sound that filled the cabin.
The loader shrugged when she asked about them.
The dispatcher claimed higher approval over her route.
Ines knew she was the one who would answer.
She filed a report and waited for silence.
The boxes grew cold as the flight continued.
Her six-year routine shattered by invisible cargo.
The drone flew into a storm of static.
She realized the route was no longer real.
The boxes opened mid-air to reveal nothing.
Only a single line of text flashed on the screen.
It read: "Route approved at a higher level."
Ines looked at the empty cargo bay.
She felt the hum stop inside the cockpit.
The drone began to drift toward the northern edge.
She checked the manifest one last time.
The four boxes were now listed as "reserved."
The dispatcher's voice echoed from the dead comms.
She realized the route was already changed.
