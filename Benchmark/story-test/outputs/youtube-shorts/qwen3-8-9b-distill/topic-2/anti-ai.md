Four sealed boxes sit in the cargo hold.
Captain Ines Varga stares at the drone.
The load sensor screams reserved cargo.
She touches the boxes. They are freezing cold.
A low hum vibrates through the floor.
She checks the flight log. The timestamps lie.
She asks the loader. He shrugs and walks away.
She calls the dispatcher. He says a higher level approved this.
Ines grips the yoke. She is the pilot.
She files a short, honest report.
Then she waits. The screen goes dark.
The boxes hum louder. She is alone in the cockpit.
