Four sealed boxes sit in the cargo hold. They are not on the manifest. They are not supposed to be there. Captain Ines Varga stares at the drone. Her six years of flying the northern corridor mean nothing now. The load sensor screams "reserved cargo." She touches the boxes. They are freezing cold. A low hum vibrates through the floor. She checks the flight log. The timestamps lie. She asks the loader. He shrugs and walks away. She calls the dispatcher. He says a higher level approved this. Ines grips the yoke. She is the pilot. She is the only one who can stop this. She files a short, honest report. Then she waits. The screen goes dark. The boxes hum louder. She is alone in the cockpit.
