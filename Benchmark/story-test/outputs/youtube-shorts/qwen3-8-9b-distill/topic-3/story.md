The rain started at the water tower. It was 6:02 AM. Dale Ransome sat in his car. The sky turned grey instantly. He had no explanation.

Dale hosted WKRV in Harrow Creek. He took every call seriously. A woman named June called at midnight. She described tomorrow's weather perfectly. She said rain would start at six. She said wind would turn east by noon. She said hail would come after three. She sounded bored. Like she was reading a shopping list.

Dale checked his forecast sheet. It said clear skies. He felt a shift in the room. The studio hummed quietly. He decided to play the call next morning.

The rain began at 6:02 AM. The wind turned east by eleven. The hail came at 3:10. Dale watched the sky turn grey. He had no explanation. He did not pretend to have one.

June never called back. The station received a flood of questions. Dale kept her letter in his drawer. He never said the word "clear" again. The rain started at the water tower.
