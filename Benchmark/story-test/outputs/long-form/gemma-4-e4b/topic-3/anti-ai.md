**ACT I: SETUP**

[SCENE START]

**INT. WKRV STUDIO - NIGHT**

The studio hums softly. Rain taps the window. DALE RANSOME (50s, weary but sharp) leans into the microphone. He hosts the midnight show. Harrow Creek listens to him.

A call connects. JUNE (O.S.) speaks. "The rain starts at the water tower at six." Dale frowns. His sheet shows clear skies.

**ACT II: CONFLICT**

**INT. WKRV STUDIO - LATER**

Dale stares at the forecast. He hesitates. The studio feels heavy. He decides to keep the call.

**EXT. TOWN WATER TOWER - MORNING**

The sky darkens. Rain hits the tower at 6:02 AM. The wind shifts east. Hail crashes at 3:10 PM. Dale watches from his car. He has no answer.

**ACT III: RESOLUTION**

**INT. WKRV STUDIO - DAY**

June never calls back. Emails flood the station. Dale finds a strange, unaddressed letter. He tucks it away. He never says "clear" on air again.

[SCENE END]
