**ACT ONE: SETUP**
Static filled the studio. Dale Ransome watched a sunny forecast. He hosted WKRV for eleven years. Every call mattered to him. June spoke calmly. She listed rain at the water tower. Wind would shift east by noon. Hail would strike just after three. Dale felt a strange chill. He checked his paper again. It still said sunny.

**ACT TWO: CONFLICT**
Dale hesitated. He considered ignoring the prediction. Curiosity pulled him forward. He played the tape. Dawn broke grey. At six minutes past six, the tower rained. Dale watched the sky turn dark. By eleven, the wind howled. At three ten, hail shattered windows. Dale sat in his car. He had no explanation. He did not pretend to know why.

**ACT THREE: RESOLUTION**
June never called back. The station flooded with emails. One letter arrived with no address. Dale kept it in his drawer. It sat next to the old sheet. He never said "clear" on air. The town trusted him again. The mystery remained unsolved. Dale drove home in silence. The storm changed everything.
