Dale Ransome hosted WKRV’s midnight show in Harrow Creek. The town relied on his voice after dark. He took every call seriously, even strange ones.  

At 12:14, June called. She said rain would start at the water tower at six. The wind would turn east by noon. Hail would come after three. She sounded calm. Bored. Like reading a list.  

Dale checked the forecast. It said clear skies. He thought. The studio hummed. At day’s end, he decided to play the call tomorrow.  

The next morning, rain began at 6:02 at the water tower. Wind turned east at eleven. Hail came at 3:10. Dale sat in his car. Watched the sky turn grey. He had no explanation. Did not pretend.  

June never called back. Emails flooded in. Questions. One letter. No return address. Dale kept it. Next to the old forecast sheet. He never said “clear” again.
