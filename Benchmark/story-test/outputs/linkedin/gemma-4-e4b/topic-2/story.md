The drone was supposed to carry only medical supplies. Captain Varga knew the northern corridor intimately. On Tuesday, the load sensor showed four unknown boxes. They were not on the manifest. The system labeled them "reserved cargo."

This unexpected cargo shifted her routine. She meticulously checked seals and flight logs. The boxes felt cold and hummed faintly. At the depot, the loader offered no explanation. The dispatcher cited higher-level approval.

Ines realized her responsibility was clear. She was the pilot. She would answer for the flight. She filed a concise, honest report. Then, she waited.

The lesson here is about operational integrity. Blind adherence to procedure can mask critical risks. When protocols feel incomplete, what is your immediate next step?
