Captain Ines Varga had flown the northern corridor for six years, knowing every waypoint by heart.

On a Tuesday run, the drone's load sensor reported four sealed boxes she had never seen. They were not on the manifest; they were marked as "reserved cargo" on the dispatch screen.

She carefully checked the seal numbers, the flight log, and wrote down every timestamp. The boxes were cold to the touch, emitting a faint, steady hum that filled the cabin.

She filed a report, short and honest. Then she sat in the cockpit, waiting for a reply that did not come.
